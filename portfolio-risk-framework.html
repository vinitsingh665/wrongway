Executive Summary

Drawdown Limits

Set clear daily, weekly, and monthly drawdown caps to prevent catastrophic losses.

Position Sizing

Risk 0.5–1% of total capital per trade. Size positions based on stop-loss distance.

Diversification

Cap exposure ≤10–15% per stock, ≤30–40% per sector. Never concentrate risk.

Portfolio Heat

Keep total open risk <3–5% of portfolio. Monitor aggregated risk across all trades.

Stop-Loss & Exits

Use initial stops, trailing stops, and aim for ≥2:1 reward-to-risk on every trade.

Recovery Plan

Step-down approach: cut sizes after 3 losses, halt after 5. Never revenge trade.

02

Per-Trade Risk & Position Sizing

Each trade's risk should be a small fixed percentage of total capital. The position size is calculated by dividing the risk amount by the distance to the stop-loss.

Position Sizing Formula
Position Size = Risk Amount (₹) Stop-Loss Distance (%)
Conservative Risk = 0.5% of ₹15L = ₹7,500 per trade
Moderate Risk = 1% of ₹15L = ₹15,000 per trade

01
Tighter stops allow larger positions (less risk per unit), provided the stop is technically justified.
02
Risk in rupees is fixed in advance. This "fixed fractional" sizing is the cornerstone of surviving drawdowns.
03
Always set your stop-loss first, then compute size so that a stop hit equals the pre-set risk.
⚠️
Never increase position because you "feel confident"; keep sizing mechanical.

04

Open-Risk Aggregation ("Portfolio Heat")

Monitor the total at-risk capital across all open trades. Cap aggregate open risk at ~2–5% of capital. This ensures no cluster of positions can wipe you out.

Portfolio Heat Meter
Cool (0-2%)
Warm (2-3.5%)
Hot (3.5-5%+)
2.5%
₹37,500

Example Calculation

5 open trades × ₹7,500 risk each = ₹37,500
Total Open Risk 2.5% of ₹15L
📏
Keep sum of individual trade risk <3–5% of portfolio. E.g. if using 0.5% per trade, no more than ~5–6 simultaneous trades.
🔗
If correlation is high (e.g. multiple stocks in same sector), consider them as one risk cluster. Adjust the count accordingly.
🚫
If total open risk limit is hit, refuse new trades until some are closed or risk is reduced. This is an explicit control to prevent overleveraging.
05

Stop-Loss, Trailing Stops & Exit Rules

🛑

Initial Stops

  • Use technical levels (swing low/high, moving average, trendline)
  • Use volatility-based stops (e.g. 1.5× ATR)
  • Place stops at least 1.5× the 14-day ATR away
  • Avoid "hope stops" – define them before entry!
📈

Trailing Stops

  • Move stop to breakeven once +2R in profit
  • Trail by fixed %, ATR, or lagging moving average
  • Best suited for trending markets
  • "Lock in profits while limiting losses"

Reward-to-Risk (R-Multiples)

Risk (1R) ₹7,500
Target Reward (2R) ₹15,000
Minimum Target ≥ 2:1 Reward-to-Risk

Pyramiding (Scaling In)

Base Position Full Size
2nd Add (trend confirmed) ½ Size
3rd Add (strong trend) ¼ Size

⚠️ Risk management becomes paramount — multiple correlated positions increase exposure. Always widen stops on earlier lots or use an overall pyramid-wide stop.

🎯
Exit Rules: Define clear profit-taking or exit criteria — fixed targets (stop at 2R), trailing exits, or exiting on technical signals. Don't exit winners for too little profit; let winners run longer.
06

Losing Streak Rules & Recovery Plan

Even a good system will have losing streaks. Pre-plan how to respond — don't wait until emotions take over.

The "Step-Down" Approach

Normal 0 Losses
Risk: 1% per trade (₹15,000)
→
Caution 3 Losses
Risk: 0.5% per trade (₹7,500)
→
HALT 5 Losses
Risk: 0.25% or STOP trading
🔍

Review & Adjust

After a losing streak, stop trading immediately and analyze. Did all trades violate stops? Did market conditions change? Check if risk rules were followed.

🚫

No Revenge Trading

Never increase size or break rules to recoup losses. Stick to fixed rules regardless of recent P&L. This is the most critical rule.

🔄

Reset Plan

If losses were due to flawed signals or unusual volatility, refine strategy or "demo-trade" before re-entering gradually.

📊
Proper risk management means even 10 consecutive losses only shrink 1–2% risk-per-trade systems by ~10–20%, which is recoverable.

📈

Scale Risk per Trade

If risk % stays constant, rupee risk rises with capital (0.5% of ₹10L = ₹5k vs 0.5% of ₹15L = ₹7.5k). Recompute at each milestone.

⚖️

Rebalance Portfolio

Periodically (quarterly), rebalance to maintain target exposures. Trim stocks that exceed exposure caps. Shift gains into cash or new ideas.

💰

Risk Budget

Add higher-risk strategies only as capital grows. Move gradually from trial phase to full allocation. Don't jump leverage proportionally.

📐

Adjust Stop Distances

Larger capital can allow wider stops without increasing % risk. But always verify stops make sense technically, not just monetary.

🧠

Update Mental Thresholds

As absolute amounts change, remind yourself of new daily/weekly loss limits (e.g. ₹45k/month was 3% of ₹15L).

08

Stress-Test Scenarios & Drawdowns

Run hypothetical scenarios to see potential drawdowns. Prevention is better than recovery.

💥

Market Crash (Systemic)

HIGH IMPACT

Sudden equity crash (–25% in a week). If 60% in stocks, crash = –15% of portfolio (₹2.25L loss). Gap risk makes stops unreliable.

Potential Loss: –15% (₹2.25L)
🌊

Volatility Spike

MEDIUM IMPACT

2× normal volatility triggers stops at 1.5× ATR. A quick –3R on a large position could mean –3% portfolio in one day.

Potential Loss: ~–10% (₹1.5L)
🎯

Concentrated Loss

LOWER IMPACT

Single position (10% of port) loses 15% = –1.5% hit. If on margin (futures double leverage), risk could double.

Potential Loss: –2% (₹30k)

09

Monitoring, Governance & Review

Implement a systematic review process — daily checklists, weekly/monthly reports, and structured governance.

☀️

Daily Checklist

  • Before market: note equity, set loss triggers
  • After close: review each trade
  • Were stops honored?
  • Record P&L and largest move against
  • Check remaining open risk
📅

Weekly Review

~30 min
  • Consolidate weekly metrics
  • Compare vs benchmarks/targets
  • Review with mentor or peer
  • Check rule enforcement
📊

Monthly Report

~1 hr
  • Starting vs ending portfolio value
  • Net P&L (%), max drawdown
  • Win rate analysis
  • Rule breaches & remedial actions

📋

Written Trading Plan

Document all risk rules like company policy. Any rule breaches must be logged and explained for accountability.

📝

Trading Journal

Record each trade's entry/exit/stop and outcome with notes. Reveals patterns like moving stops or overtrading.

Sources & References

Risk guidelines are drawn from trading education and industry practices. Trading educators emphasize small per-trade risk (≈1%) and strict loss limits.
