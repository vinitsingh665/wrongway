How to Play

🎯

Objective

Be the first player to reach the opposite side of the board. Blue starts at the bottom and must reach the top. Red starts at the top and must reach the bottom.

🏃

Moving

On your turn, click your piece, then click an adjacent cell (up, down, left, or right) to move. You can't move diagonally or through walls.

🧱

Barricades

Instead of moving, you can place a wall. Click a horizontal (━) or vertical (┃) wall tool, then click a grid intersection to place it. Each wall blocks 2 cells.

⚠️

Key Rule

You cannot completely block a player's path to their goal. There must always be at least one route open for both players.
